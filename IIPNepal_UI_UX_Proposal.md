# UI/UX Design Proposal: IIPNepal

**Date:** October 8, 2026 | **Prepared by:** Kaushal Rai | **For:** IIPNepal

---

## 1. Project Objective
Redesign the IIPNepal website to function as an immersive, oversized editorial art-book. Building upon the completed landing page, the goal is to create a premium, user-centric platform that prioritizes stunning full-bleed photography, whisper-light typography, and cinematic motion to drive course discovery and student enrollment.

## 2. Design & Motion System
**Visual Identity & Layout:**
- **The Canvas:** A warm, printed-paper feel using a Bone White background (`#fffef7`)—never pure sterile white.
- **Editorial Typography:** Utilizing whisper-thin, ultra-light sans-serifs scaled up to massive poster sizes with tightly carved letter-spacing.
- **Sharp & Soft Contrasts:** Sharp, unrounded edges (0px radius) for full-bleed photography and project cards, contrasted with perfectly rounded pill shapes (1440px radius) for interactive buttons and navigation controls.
- **Color Restraint:** A monochromatic Ink Black on Bone White base, punctuated by rationed, highly saturated accents (Signal Yellow, Mint Wash, Magenta Bloom) for editorial tagging, badges, and interactions.

**Advanced Motion Choreography:**
- **Cinematic Reveals:** Horizontal strip loading sequences that stretch and unfurl vertically to reveal full viewport photography.
- **Staggered Motion:** Multi-phase staggered typography reveals, pinned horizontal scroll galleries, and subtle parallax effects using custom easing for a weightless, frictionless feel.

## 3. Scope & Deliverables
**Scope:** Complete UI/UX design featuring advanced layout mapping and motion choreography. *(Please note: This scope is exclusively for UI/UX Design; backend engineering and full-stack software development are not included).*  
**Deliverables:** High-fidelity, responsive designs (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), detailed animation specifications, and a developer-ready UI style guide.

**Included Page Templates & Views (9 Total):**
1. **Home / Landing** *(Completed)* — Editorial poster hero, What We Do, Polaroid courses showcase, admissions timeline, pinned horizontal student portfolio showcase, alumni testimonials, brand marquee strip, news & perspectives, and architectural contact lockup.
2. **About Us** — Editorial manifesto statement, institutional pillars & kinetic disciplines marquee, academic leadership dossier, 7-step admissions playbook, and prospectus downloads.
3. **Courses Directory** — Comprehensive curriculum catalog, filterable by discipline/tier, with course metadata and admissions callouts.
4. **Course Details (Dynamic Template)** — In-depth syllabus view featuring course hero, curriculum breakdown, course essentials (gear, prerequisites), and integrated tuition & FAQ section.
5. **Portfolio Directory** — Exhibition-grade student & alumni archive featuring category filters (Documentary, Studio, Fine Art, Landscape) and curatorial manifesto.
6. **Portfolio Case Study (Dynamic Template)** — Immersive editorial project view featuring full-bleed gallery showcase, photographer story dossier, and project next/previous navigation.
7. **Admissions & Course Enrollment** — Multi-step student enrollment dossier application form with course selection, applicant background, and portfolio link submission.
8. **Student Portal Login** — Dedicated student access view and interactive authentication modal.
9. **Contact & Admissions** — Direct communication channels, studio & darkroom location (Jyoti Bhawan, Kanti Path) with interactive map graphic, and admissions enquiry form.

*(Note: Features such as "How It Works" and "FAQ" are seamlessly integrated within their respective core pages—About/Home and Course Details—rather than redundant standalone pages).*
