**Date:** October 5, 2026 | **Prepared by:** Kaushal Rai | **For:** IIPNepal

---

## 1. Project Objective
Redesign the IIPNepal website to function as an immersive, oversized editorial art-book. Building upon the completed landing page, the goal is to create a premium, user-centric platform that prioritizes stunning full-bleed photography, whisper-light typography, and cinematic motion to drive course discovery and enrollment.

## 2. Design & Motion System
**Visual Identity & Layout:**
- **The Canvas:** A warm, printed-paper feel using a Bone White background—never pure white.
- **Editorial Typography:** Utilizing whisper-thin, ultra-light sans-serifs scaled up to massive poster sizes with tightly carved letter-spacing.
- **Sharp & Soft Contrasts:** Sharp, unrounded edges for full-bleed images and project cards, contrasted with perfectly rounded pill shapes for interactive buttons and navigation.
- **Color Restraint:** A monochromatic Ink Black on Bone White base, punctuated by rationed, highly saturated accents (e.g., Signal Yellow, Mint Wash, Magenta) for editorial tagging and interactions.

**Advanced Animation Choreography:**
- **Cinematic Reveals:** Horizontal strip loading sequences that stretch and unfurl vertically to reveal full viewport photography.
- **Staggered Motion:** Multi-phase staggered typography reveals, pinned scroll interactions, and subtle parallax effects using custom easing for a weightless, frictionless feel.

## 3. Scope & Deliverables

**Deliverables:** High-fidelity, responsive designs (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), detailed animation specifications, and a developer-ready style guide.
